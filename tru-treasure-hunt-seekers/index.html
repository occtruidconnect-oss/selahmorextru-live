How to Play

1. Read the clue. Story clues, "Who am I?", prophecy and wisdom clues — each one points to a real moment in Scripture.

2. Hunt the passage. The open book holds the real Bible text. One word in it is the treasure. Tap words to test them — wrong taps just wiggle and whisper warmer or colder.

3. Use the hint ladder freely. First a warm glow gathers near the treasure, then the first letter is revealed, then — with grace — the word is shown to you. Hints cost a few bonus gems, never your dignity.

4. Dig deeper. Every find opens the full verse, a Dig Deeper note with history, an original Hebrew or Greek word, a cross-reference, and a Treasure Truth to carry with you.

5. Fill your Vault. Found verses and gems collect in your Treasure Vault for this session — tap any verse there to hear it read aloud.
