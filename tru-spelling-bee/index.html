HOW THE SHOWDOWN WORKS

  • 1Buzzy the Bee says your word out loud. Tap Hear the Word anytime, ask for the Definition, or hear it In a Sentence — just like a real spelling bee.
  • 2Spell it by tapping the giant letter tiles (or typing on a keyboard). Spell it right on the first try and your token zooms ahead — bigger words move you farther!
  • 3Grace Card: miss once and grace steps in — you get a hint and one more try. Nobody gets stung out of this game.
  • 4Honey Drops protect you. Miss with no second try and you lose a drop; lose a drop with an empty jar and you slide back one space. Three correct in a row refills the jar!
  • 5The Trophy Gate: reach the end and one Championship Word stands between you and the trophy. Spell it — and the confetti falls!
