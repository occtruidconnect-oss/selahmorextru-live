About

TRU Identity Ministry

Selah More is the interactive home of TRU Identity Ministry — a trauma-informed, faith-driven community where everybody belongs. Here, faith meets real life: through games, stories, music, and honest conversation.

The ministry was founded by Minister Jade “TRU” Wynne, an ordained minister and spoken-word artist building spaces where the overlooked are seen, the hurting are held, and the truth is spoken in love.
