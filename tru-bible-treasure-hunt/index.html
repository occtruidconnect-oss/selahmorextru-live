Bible Treasure Hunt

Every hunt hides a treasure word inside a real Bible verse. Read the clue, open the page, and tap the word when you find it — dig up gems, fill your treasure chest, and discover how much God loves you!

"Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path." PSALM 119:105 (WEB)

How to Play

Three easy steps — grown-ups, play right along!

1

Read the clue. A question about a Bible story — tap "Hear It" if you want it read out loud.

2

Hunt the page. The answer is hiding as ONE word inside the verse. Read carefully and tap it!

3

Dig up treasure. Every find earns a gem for your chest — and a treasure truth to keep in your heart.

Who's Hunting?

1 to 4 explorers. Playing together? Pass the tablet to each hunter when their turn banner shows — a curtain keeps every hunt a surprise.
